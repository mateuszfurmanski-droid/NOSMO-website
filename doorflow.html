Focused construction module

Prototype / demonstrator

DoorFlow.

A focused door and fire-door workflow connecting plans, schedules, door objects, lifecycle state, defects, evidence and approvals so the record follows the actual door rather than a spreadsheet thread.

See workflowNexus context
InputsPDF drawings, schedules and site evidence.
LifecycleInspection, installation and replacement workflows are part of the underlying DoorFlow direction.
EvidencePhotos, defects, validation and approval state remain attached to the relevant door record.

Workflow

Plan → schedule → match → execute → evidence → approve.

Drawing and schedule match

Bring door references, levels, rooms, ratings and required hardware into one object-level context.

Inspection / installation / replacement

Record the relevant lifecycle workflow rather than treating every door as a generic checklist row.

Defects and evidence

Attach photos, notes, validation results and outstanding actions to the correct door record.

Approval gating

Completion state can depend on required evidence and approvals instead of a manual status colour alone.

Handover record

Keep lifecycle evidence with the asset history so site information is not lost at project close.

Nexus pattern

DoorFlow demonstrates the wider object + evidence + timeline pattern that can extend to other specialist packages.

Status: DoorFlow is presented as a prototype/demonstrator and active technical direction, not as a certified production fire-door compliance system.

Integration layer

Keep the useful tools. Connect the missing context.

Nexus is an overlay. It is designed to carry relationships, permissions, actions, evidence and project memory between NOSMO modules and existing systems.

Construction and workforce platforms

Integration direction includes systems such as Work Wallet, Procore, Dalux, Viewpoint and CompanyCam, with controlled access rather than duplicated ownership.

Communication and people

Email, WhatsApp, SMS and contacts can become action routes around the correct person, project and permission boundary.

BIM, files and specialist sources

BIM and coordination tools, document storage, Drive and specialist applications remain authoritative sources where they already perform well.

Status boundary: these are integration targets and product directions. The public site does not claim that every connector is already live.

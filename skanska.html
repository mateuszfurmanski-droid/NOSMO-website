Working demonstrators · synthetic demo data

Two use cases. One connected lifecycle.

NOSMO prepared two Nexus demonstrators around SKANSKA-facing scenarios: circular construction for Residential and commercial building operations for Property. Together they show how project information can remain connected from construction through asset operation, maintenance, replacement and reuse.

Demo 01 · Residential / SPARK context

Circular Asset & Material Layer

Object Cards for materials, products, components, assets and equipment; provenance and evidence; lifecycle and maintenance context; human circular decisions; reporting without fabricated carbon values.

Demo 02 · Commercial Property

Building Operations & Asset Graph

Building → floor → space → asset → issue → task → evidence → approval → replacement → reuse → ESG. The AHU-04 scenario demonstrates the workflow end to end.

Launch Property demo
Demonstrator boundary: both demos use explicitly synthetic demonstration data and are intended to show product capability and workflow direction. They do not represent a live SKANSKA deployment, measured SKANSKA performance or an approved SKANSKA integration.

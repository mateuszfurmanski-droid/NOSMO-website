NOSMO Construction Hardware Development

R&D / prototype

EXO-01.

A construction exoskeleton direction built around practical load support: passive and mechanical first, modular tool support, and onboard power only where the task requires it.

ArchitectureTechnical collaboration

Gen-1 direction

Assist the worker. Prove the mechanics first.

EXO-01 is an early engineering track, not a certified commercial product. The first useful version should solve specific heavy repetitive tasks before adding unnecessary complexity.

Passive / mechanical first

Use structural load paths, springs, counterbalance or other mechanical assistance before relying on powered joints.

Modular tool support

Support interchangeable task modules and heavy tools rather than building one fixed-purpose suit.

Onboard power where required

Carry power on the frame for supported tools or subsystems when a tether would make the system impractical.

Bricklaying

Explore reduction of repetitive load and awkward holding positions around material and tool handling.

Road / ground work

Target heavy repetitive tools and sustained posture where frame support could materially reduce worker load.

Heavy repetitive construction

Prioritise real site tasks with measurable ergonomic and productivity value.

Development gates

Concept → rig → task prototype → measured trials.

Load paths, range of motion, fail-safe behaviour, tool mounting, balance and human factors need physical validation before any product claim.

Software connection

Nexus and Worker integration can come later.

Digital setup, inspection or work records may connect to NOSMO software where useful, but the hardware must first prove its mechanical value independently.

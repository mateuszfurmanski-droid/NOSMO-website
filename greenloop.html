R&D / pilot development

GreenLoop.

A controlled circular-material track focused on selected clean polymer streams, practical reprocessing and useful manufactured outputs rather than mixed or heavily contaminated waste.

Material pipelineDiscuss a material stream
Priority streamTPU production waste and pallets, plus other clean identifiable B2B feedstock.
PolymersTPU, PP, HDPE and ABS where source quality and process compatibility are controlled.
OutputsGranulate, extrusion trials, filament, simple parts and formed products.

Material pipeline

Source → Sort → Process → Granulate → Extrude → Print / Form → Reuse.

Each batch should retain a traceable material identity and process record so the manufacturing route can be improved rather than treated as generic recycling.

01 SOURCEClean, known B2B polymer feedstock.
02 SORTSeparate polymer type and reject unsuitable material.
03 PROCESSClean, shred and dry to a controlled feed condition.
04 GRANULATEPrepare repeatable material for downstream use.
05 EXTRUDEPellet, filament or other controlled extrusion trials.
06 PRINT / FORMPrototype, print or thermally form useful parts.
07 REUSEReturn material value into a new practical application.

TPU waste streams

Current interest includes clean TPU production waste where material identity, contamination level and supply consistency can be verified.

PP / HDPE / ABS

Use only technically suitable, identifiable streams with enough consistency for meaningful process control and repeatability.

Simple manufactured parts

Start with useful prototypes, fixtures and low-complexity parts before attempting demanding structural applications.

Filament / extrusion

Develop practical granulate and extrusion knowledge with documented temperatures, drying conditions and batch behaviour.

Forming routes

Explore print and thermal-forming routes where recycled polymer properties suit the geometry and duty.

Composite / fibre trials

Hemp, flax, basalt or other reinforcement remains a later test direction only where engineering data justifies it.

Feedstock rule: GreenLoop is not positioned around random dirty waste. The preferred starting point is a known, clean and traceable material stream that can be processed consistently.

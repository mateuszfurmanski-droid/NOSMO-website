R&D / pilot development

GreenLoop.

A controlled circular-material track for known, clean polymer streams and useful manufactured outputs. The starting point is process discipline, not mixed or heavily contaminated waste.

Material pipelineDiscuss a material stream
PriorityClean TPU production waste and identifiable B2B polymer streams.
PolymersTPU, PP, HDPE and ABS where identity and process compatibility are controlled.

Material pipeline

Source → Sort → Process → Granulate → Extrude → Print / Form → Reuse.

Each batch should retain a known material identity and a controlled process history.

01 SOURCEKnown, clean B2B feedstock.
02 SORTSeparate polymer and reject unsuitable feed.
03 PROCESSClean, shred and dry.
04 GRANULATEPrepare repeatable feedstock.
05 EXTRUDERun controlled extrusion trials.
06 PRINT / FORMMake useful test parts or formed products.
07 REUSEReturn material value to a practical use.

TPU streams

Clean TPU production waste is a priority when source, contamination level and supply consistency can be verified.

PP / HDPE / ABS

Use identifiable feedstock with enough consistency for meaningful process control and testing.

Useful outputs

Start with granulate, extrusion trials, filament, fixtures, simple parts and formed products before demanding applications.

Next material research

Composite and fibre trials when justified.

Later work can examine polymer and fibre combinations where the material, safety and end-use case are understood. It is a research direction, not a current production claim.

Feedstock rule

Known, clean and traceable wins.

GreenLoop is not positioned around random dirty waste. A repeatable route starts with a source that can be identified, handled and processed responsibly.

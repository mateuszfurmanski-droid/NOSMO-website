Work Zone

Your device becomes a work environment.

Work Zone is the operational face of NEXUS. Instead of asking a worker to search through a normal phone or laptop full of unrelated apps, it presents only the tools, tasks, documents, contacts and project information relevant to the work being done.

Focused work desktop

NEXUS can act as the working layer over the device: project tools stay visible while distractions and irrelevant applications stay outside the work context.

Shared project cloud

Managers, site teams and office users work from the same live project context. Updates, evidence, documents and status changes can move through the project without manual re-entry.

Instant information flow

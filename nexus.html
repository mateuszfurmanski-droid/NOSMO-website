Existing software and communication

Nexus is the connective layer, not a forced replacement.

Integration should preserve useful source systems while adding the missing relationship between the person, project, task, evidence and decision.

Construction platforms

Targets include Work Wallet, Procore, Dalux, Viewpoint, CompanyCam and other established workforce, project and evidence tools.

Communication routes

Email, WhatsApp, SMS and contacts can be connected to the correct person or project through a controlled pre-action layer.

BIM, storage and specialist tools

Model-authoring, coordination, Drive, document storage and specialist systems can remain authoritative sources connected through Nexus.

Current boundary: integration readiness differs by system. These are product directions unless a specific connector is identified as live in a released module.

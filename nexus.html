Platform in development

NOSMO Nexus.

Nexus is being built as the connective operating layer around construction work: people, projects, files, tasks, decisions, project history and specialist systems linked into one persistent context.

Explore architectureOpen DoorFlow
PrincipleOverlay and connect existing workflows rather than forcing a construction company to replace every specialist tool.
MemoryPreserve who, what, where, when and why around project actions instead of leaving knowledge scattered across folders and messages.
AIAsk Nexus should help retrieve, explain and prepare actions while keeping provenance, uncertainty and human approval visible.
Core Nexus objects

The project becomes a connected system, not another folder tree.

These are the current platform concepts. They describe the intended operating model, not a claim that every capability is production-complete today.

People / Person Cards

Identity, roles, skills, permissions, relationships and relevant work context.

Projects

Shared project worlds linking people, locations, work, evidence and source systems.

Documents

Drawings, PDFs, schedules, certificates and project files attached to the objects they actually describe.

Tasks

Assignable work with requirements, dependencies, documents, location and evidence expectations.

Timeline

A chronological record of meaningful changes, actions, approvals, evidence and project events.

Project Memory

Durable history of what happened, who was involved, what was approved and what changed.

Knowledge Graph

Relationships between people, companies, locations, documents, assets, tasks and decisions.

Ask Nexus

Natural-language access to authorised project context, with human confirmation for important external actions.

Construction integrations

Ask Nexus

Stop searching. Start asking.

The long-term interaction model is not a chatbot floating above disconnected data. Ask Nexus is intended to operate on the same authorised objects, relationships and timeline as the rest of the platform.

Current maturity

Architecture + modules + active development

Nexus currently combines a substantial product architecture/documentation base with working demonstrations and focused modules. The wider operating layer remains under development and should be evaluated as such.

Product status: this page describes the current Nexus direction and active build architecture. It does not claim that every integration, AI action or platform module is commercially deployed.

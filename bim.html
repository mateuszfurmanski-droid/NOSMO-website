BIM / MEP direction

Specialist systems in project context.

NOSMO BIM is designed to connect objects to work and evidence without pretending to replace the model-authoring or coordination systems.

Field technology R&D

Guidance can move to the field.

Spatial computing and field interfaces are future research directions. They become product work only where a real site workflow supports them.

Integration boundary

BIM authoring and coordination remain specialist work.

External BIM partners and existing platforms can create or coordinate the model. Nexus holds the relationship, readiness, evidence, approvals and project memory around real execution.

Future specialist modules

Add another module only when the pattern repeats.

Future trade applications must earn their place through a genuine object, workflow and evidence need. They are not public products until that path is clear.

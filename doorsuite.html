DoorSuite.

DoorSuite connects plans and Excel schedules to the complete door lifecycle: installation, inspection, replacement, defects, photos, approvals and handover.

Door workflowWorkSuite overview
InputsPDF drawings, Excel door schedules and controlled site evidence.
Door objectOne record can carry its location, specification, history and current status.
OutcomeEvidence-led inspection and handover, linked back to Nexus project memory.

DoorSuite workflow

Plan → schedule → object → work → evidence → sign-off.

The same door record should stay with the work whether the task is installation, inspection, replacement or a defect correction.

Plan and schedule matching

Connect door references, levels, rooms, ratings, hardware and requirements before field work begins.

Installation, inspection and replacement

Keep all lifecycle actions on the relevant door instead of splitting the history across disconnected sheets.

Defects, photos and approval

Record evidence, remedial work and approval decisions against the correct object for a defensible handover.

Product status: DoorSuite is an active NOSMO WorkSuite direction. It is not represented as a completed commercial production platform. BIM object and location references can connect through Nexus where useful.

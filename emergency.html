NOSMO Emergency.

A mobile-first, offline-capable emergency assistance PWA for UK use. It is deliberately independent from Work, Agency and Nexus Core so critical guidance does not rely on another NOSMO product or backend.

PriorityPotentially life-threatening situations prioritise 999 / 112 and emergency service operator instructions.
OfflineCritical protocol data is stored locally and does not depend on a NOSMO backend.
PrivacyNo account is required for the V1 core; location is requested only after explicit user action.

Emergency activation

Large emergency controls, scene-safety prompts and prominent 999 / 112 actions.

Core guidance

CPR/AED, severe bleeding, choking, stroke, suspected heart attack, anaphylaxis, seizure and other emergency routes.

Local routing

Free-text symptom routing is designed so it cannot suppress life-threatening escalation.

17 UK-relevant languages

Critical emergency paths use local language resources, with reviewed-English fallback where extended translation is incomplete.

Optional device support

Voice input, read-aloud, haptics and user-requested geolocation can be used where the device supports them.

Source governance

Guidance provenance is maintained against UK authoritative sources including NHS, Resuscitation Council UK, British Red Cross and St John Ambulance.

Safety status: Emergency remains in release-candidate validation. It is not a replacement for 999, 112, paramedics, doctors, qualified first aiders or emergency medical services. Final public medical-use release depends on device, clinical-content and translation review gates.

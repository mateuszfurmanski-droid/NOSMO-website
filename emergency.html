Emergency.

Emergency is built into the NOSMO Workforce package for fast access when something goes wrong. Its safety core remains offline-capable so critical guidance does not depend on the rest of the Workforce runtime.

Back to Workforce
PriorityPotentially life-threatening situations prioritise 999 / 112 and emergency service operator instructions.
Offline coreCritical protocol data can operate without a NOSMO backend.
PrivacyLocation is requested only after explicit user action.

Emergency activation

Large emergency controls, scene-safety prompts and prominent 999 / 112 actions.

Core guidance

CPR/AED, severe bleeding, choking, stroke, suspected heart attack, anaphylaxis, seizure and other emergency routes.

Workforce access

Emergency remains available as a built-in Workforce safety function while preserving an isolated safety-critical core.

Safety status: Emergency is not a replacement for 999, 112, paramedics, doctors, qualified first aiders or emergency medical services.

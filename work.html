Core worker layer

Everything a worker repeatedly has to prove, find or send.

Identity

Worker Card

Skills, trade, profile, work history and a portable worker-facing record designed to move between jobs and agencies.

Documents

Credentials pack

CSCS/ECS-style cards, certificates, training, CV, ID, right-to-work evidence and other job documents in one controlled place.

Work status

Availability

Worker-controlled availability state that can be shared into consented recruiter workflows instead of being repeatedly requested by message.

Jobs

Job workflow

Job discovery and application context connected to the same worker record rather than scattered across separate apps and inboxes.

AI

Ask Nexus

A conversational layer intended to help the worker find information, prepare actions and use their work context without pretending that unverified data is true.

Communication

Apps layer

Permission-controlled handoff into communication tools with the correct work context, contact and prepared message where supported.

Connected product

NOSMO Agency

Recruiters can work with a recruiter-safe view of worker information, requests and matching without taking ownership of the worker's private record.

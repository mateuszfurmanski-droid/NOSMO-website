NOSMO Work.

A mobile-first construction work layer built around worker ownership: identity, credentials, documents, availability, jobs, contacts and controlled communication in one portable workspace.

Worker identity

Person Card evolved into a practical Worker Card.

The identity layer is not a public social profile. It is a worker-controlled record of relevant work information and evidence that can be reused across jobs and agencies.

Documents

One controlled place for worker documents, certificates and application material, with progressive disclosure rather than a crowded file dump.

Availability

Worker-controlled Available, Busy and Ready-on-date state can feed approved recruiter-safe Agency connections.

Jobs + Search V2

Job search and application context sit against the same worker record. Live search is implemented but remains under production observation.

Contacts + intake

Explicit imports and Android Share can bring job/contact information from other apps through review rather than background scanning.

Apps layer

Work tools and external services can be surfaced from the worker workspace while keeping secondary services collapsed until needed.

Ask Nexus

AI-assisted search and action preparation use approved work context. Automatic sending or submission is not implied.
